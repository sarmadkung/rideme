# 118 — Safety Data Privacy & Retention

## Objective
Protect sensitive trust, identity, incident and location information.

## Data Classification
Classify data such as:
```text
PUBLIC
INTERNAL
SENSITIVE
HIGHLY_SENSITIVE
```

## Access
Use least privilege and role-specific access.

## Retention
Separate policies for:
- identity documents
- live location
- historical location
- incidents
- fraud signals
- audit events

Do not retain everything indefinitely.

## Deletion
Where legally and operationally possible:
- delete
- anonymize
- aggregate

Preserve legally required records under controlled access.

## Definition of Done
Sensitive safety data has explicit access, retention and deletion policies.
