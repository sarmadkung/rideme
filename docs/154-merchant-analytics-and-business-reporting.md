# 154 — Merchant Analytics & Business Reporting

## Objective
Help merchants understand sales and fulfillment performance.

## Metrics
- orders
- gross sales
- net sales
- average order value
- cancellation rate
- preparation time
- delivery time
- refunds
- settlement

## Reports
Support:
```text
daily
weekly
monthly
custom range
```

## Reconciliation
Financial reports must reconcile against the ledger/settlement system.

## Definition of Done
Merchants can analyze operations and reconcile reported financial totals.
