# Logistics Platform — Project Vision

## Working Positioning

**A unified transportation and logistics marketplace that connects people, businesses, and goods with the right vehicle at the right price and time.**

The platform is not positioned as another generic ride-hailing app. Its core abstraction is:

> **Vehicle → Capabilities → Jobs → Intelligent Dispatch**

A single registered vehicle can participate in multiple compatible services.

## Core Vehicle Types

- Motorcycle
- Rickshaw
- Car
- Loader
- Suzuki pickup
- Shehzore
- Mazda
- Truck

## Core Services

### Initial
1. Passenger rides
2. Parcel/document delivery
3. Small cargo / moving

### Later
4. Merchant delivery
5. Grocery delivery through existing merchants
6. Scheduled recurring logistics
7. Return-load matching
8. Intercity freight
9. Logistics API

## Product Principle

Do not build separate platforms for rides, parcels, groceries and cargo.

Build one **Job Platform** with specialized job types.

## North-Star Metric

**Completed profitable jobs per active vehicle per day**

Supporting metrics:

- Driver net income per online hour
- Successful fulfillment rate
- Empty kilometers / total kilometers
- Contribution margin per job
- Active vehicles
- Jobs per vehicle
- Driver retention
- Customer repeat rate

## Strategic Thesis

The strongest differentiation is not the UI. It is the network:

1. More vehicle types
2. More job types
3. Better vehicle utilization
4. Fewer empty kilometers
5. Better driver earnings
6. More reliable fulfillment
7. More merchants
8. More demand
9. Better dispatch data
10. Better economics

## Launch Principle

Start with one city and a constrained service area. Build supply density before broad consumer acquisition.

Recommended first wedge:

**Bike + Rickshaw + Car + Loader/Suzuki → Ride + Parcel + Small Cargo**

Do not launch a warehouse-based grocery operation initially.
