## SAFE-016 — E2E
```text
Driver Verification
 → Vehicle Approval
 → Passenger Books Ride
 → Active Trip
 → Risk/Safety Event
 → SOS/Incident
 → Operations Escalation
 → Resolution
 → Audit
```

## Phase 9 Exit Criteria
The platform can verify participants, protect active trips, detect suspicious behavior, handle emergencies and incidents, enforce platform policies and maintain auditable trust/safety records.
