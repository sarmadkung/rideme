# 153 — Driver Analytics & Earnings

## Objective
Give drivers useful performance and earnings information without encouraging unsafe behavior.

## Metrics
- completed jobs
- cancellation rate
- acceptance rate
- online time
- active time
- gross earnings
- fees
- net earnings
- tips where applicable

## Efficiency
Use balanced metrics.

Do not reward dangerous driving or excessive speed.

## Driver Dashboard
```text
Today
This Week
This Month
Earnings
Jobs
Performance
```

## Definition of Done
Drivers can understand earnings and operational performance from reliable financial and job records.
