# 110 — Trip Sharing & Trusted Contacts

## Objective
Allow a passenger to share relevant active-trip information with trusted contacts.

## Shared Information
Depending on policy:
- driver first name/photo
- vehicle identifier
- pickup
- destination
- live location
- ETA
- trip status

## Access
Use temporary, revocable sharing tokens rather than permanent public links.

## Expiration
Trip-sharing access should expire automatically when the trip ends.

## Privacy
Do not expose:
- unnecessary driver personal data
- private historical routes
- unrelated trip information

## Definition of Done
A customer can securely share an active trip and the access expires automatically.
