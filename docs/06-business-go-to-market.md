# Go-To-Market & Launch Plan

## Launch Philosophy

Do not launch nationwide.

Launch one city, then one dense operating area.

## Recommended Initial City

Lahore is a strong candidate because of:
- population density
- commercial activity
- e-commerce
- retail
- restaurants
- universities
- residential demand
- intra-city logistics

Validate this choice with field research and unit economics before committing.

## Initial Operating Area

Start with approximately 5–8 connected zones.

Optimize for:
- high driver density
- high order density
- short pickup ETAs
- repeat customers
- manageable support operations

## Supply-First Strategy

Pre-launch target:
- 300–500 verified vehicles
- 250 bikes
- 100 rickshaws
- 75 cars
- 50 loaders/Suzuki

These are planning targets, not fixed requirements.

## Merchant Acquisition

Target:
- Grocery stores
- Pharmacies
- Restaurants
- Instagram/social sellers
- Small e-commerce merchants
- Electronics/furniture shops

Offer:
- Delivery without hiring drivers
- Tracking
- COD
- Proof of delivery
- Bulk upload
- Scheduled deliveries

## Customer Acquisition

Initial channels:
- Merchant-driven customer acquisition
- Driver referrals
- Local area campaigns
- Referral program
- WhatsApp
- Community partnerships

Avoid expensive mass marketing before fulfillment is reliable.

## Driver Acquisition

Channels:
- Driver referrals
- Vehicle markets/workshops
- Fuel stations
- Local commercial hubs
- Existing driver communities
- Fleet owners

Core pitch:

> Earn from multiple job types with one vehicle.

## Driver Retention

Offer:
- Transparent earnings
- Estimated net earnings
- Fair cancellation compensation
- Fast settlements
- Multi-service jobs
- Route-compatible jobs
- Driver support
- Performance insights

## Launch Phases

### Phase 0 — Discovery
4–6 weeks

Interview:
- 50 drivers
- 20 merchants
- 20 customers
- 10 cargo operators
- 10 e-commerce merchants

### Phase 1 — MVP
3–4 months

Ride + parcel + small cargo.

### Phase 2
Months 5–7

Merchant portal, COD, scheduled jobs, return loads.

### Phase 3
Months 8–12

Grocery marketplace, recurring B2B, API, advanced optimization.

### Phase 4

Intercity and freight.

## Launch Gates

Do not expand a zone until:
- fulfillment rate is healthy
- driver retention is healthy
- average pickup time is acceptable
- contribution margin is improving
- support load is manageable
