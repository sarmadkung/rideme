# 111 — Ratings, Reviews & Quality

## Objective
Create a fair feedback system for customers, drivers and merchants.

## Rating
Example:
```text
1–5 stars
```

Allow structured reasons such as:
- driving
- cleanliness
- punctuality
- communication
- package handling

## Review Rules
Prevent:
- duplicate submissions
- rating manipulation
- retaliation where policy requires protection
- reviews from unauthorized users

## Quality Signals
Calculate separately:
```text
rating
cancellation rate
completion rate
complaints
late rate
incident rate
```

Do not reduce trust to one rating number.

## Definition of Done
Ratings contribute to quality monitoring without becoming an unreliable single source of truth.
