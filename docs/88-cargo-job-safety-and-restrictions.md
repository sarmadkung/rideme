# 88 — Cargo Safety & Restrictions

## Objective
Prevent unsuitable or prohibited cargo from entering the normal logistics flow.

## Required Information
Depending on service:
- item category
- weight
- dimensions
- declared value
- handling requirements

## Restricted Items
The platform should maintain configurable business/legal restrictions and require manual review where appropriate.

Do not assume every parcel is eligible for every vehicle/service.

## Driver Visibility
Only provide information needed to perform the job safely and lawfully.

## High-Value Cargo
Possible additional controls:
- stronger POD
- OTP
- identity verification
- limited reassignment
- operations monitoring

## Damage
Allow driver/customer to report package condition at pickup and delivery.

## Definition of Done
Restricted, oversized or high-risk cargo follows explicit policy instead of silently entering normal dispatch.
