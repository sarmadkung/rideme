## Competitor Lessons

### Bykea
Strengths:
- Strong local brand
- Multi-service positioning
- Bikes, rickshaws, cars
- Parcel/COD/shopping

Opportunity:
- Differentiate on vehicle identity, transparent pricing, driver economics, support, cargo and network optimization.

### inDrive
Strengths:
- Customer/driver price negotiation
- Strong driver sense of control
- Large ride network

Opportunity:
- Avoid unrestricted race-to-the-bottom bidding.
- Introduce a fair-price range and cancellation compensation.

### Yango
Strengths:
- Strong dispatch technology
- Mobility + logistics
- Business/cargo capabilities

Opportunity:
- Differentiate on driver profitability, transparency, local operations and broader vehicle capability.

### foodpanda
Strengths:
- Huge consumer network
- Grocery/food marketplace
- Merchant ecosystem

Opportunity:
- Do not fight grocery head-on initially.
- Become the logistics layer for local merchants.

### RideAll
Strengths:
- Commercial vehicles
- Cargo/house shifting
- Vehicle-specific booking

Opportunity:
- Customer should describe the cargo/job; platform should recommend the appropriate vehicle.
- Add rides + parcel + cargo into one network.
- Build return-load matching.

### Krave Mart
Strengths:
- Quick commerce
- Managed inventory and fulfillment

Opportunity:
- Avoid owning inventory initially.
- Use merchant inventory and provide delivery infrastructure.

## Core Gap

Existing products are mostly **service-centric**.

Our product should be **job-centric and vehicle-centric**.

Customer asks:
> What needs to move?

Platform determines:
> Which vehicle should perform the job?

Driver asks:
> How can I maximize my earnings today?

Platform determines:
> Which jobs produce the best route-compatible income?
