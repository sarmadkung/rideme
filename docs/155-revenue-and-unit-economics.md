# 155 — Revenue & Unit Economics

## Objective
Measure whether each service and transaction is economically viable.

## Revenue
```text
Gross Customer Charges
- Refunds
- Discounts funded by platform
= Net Customer Revenue
```

## Contribution
Consider:
- driver/provider payout
- payment fees
- map costs
- communication costs
- support cost allocation
- incentives

## Metrics
- revenue per trip
- contribution per trip
- revenue per delivery
- cost per order
- customer acquisition cost
- lifetime value

## Important
Keep accounting ledger values authoritative. Analytics may aggregate them but should not become the source of financial truth.

## Definition of Done
Management can evaluate unit economics by service, geography and cohort.
