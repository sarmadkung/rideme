# Product Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Validation

Duration: 4–6 weeks

Deliverables:
- Competitor research
- Driver interviews
- Merchant interviews
- Customer interviews
- City/zone selection
- Pricing research
- Unit economics model
- Legal/compliance review
- Prototype

Exit criteria:
- Clear launch wedge
- Confirmed pain points
- Initial supply commitments
- Initial merchant commitments
- Viable unit economics hypothesis
